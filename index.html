About me

Hi, I'm Giorgio.

I'm Italian and I live in Berlin. By day I work in university admissions, where I built the dashboards and reports a whole team runs on every week. I co-founded HomeSettled.

I also build for myself: a catalogue that merges my record collection from many stores into one clean record, and Pitch Riders, a tool that helps DJs find new vinyl. I DJ as Flight Mode and Pulp Friction.

What I do best is take a slow, manual process and turn it into a system that runs by itself, with a person still in control. You don't need to speak tech. That part is my job.
