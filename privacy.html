3. Fonts and video

All fonts and the background video are stored on this website. Your browser does not connect to Google or any other outside server to load them.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising tools. Some demo pages remember small settings in your own browser; this data never leaves your device.

5. Demos

The dashboard and company-brain demos use invented data and run only in your browser. Nothing you type into them is sent to me or anyone else.

6. Contact by email

If you write to me, I use your email address and message only to answer you and, if we work together, to carry out our agreement. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR. I delete the emails when they are no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep them (for example for invoices).

7. Feedback form

I ask people I have worked with for feedback through a Google Form (Google Ireland Ltd., Ireland). The form collects your name, role, company, the type of work and your feedback, and it states that the feedback will be shown on this website. Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. You can withdraw your consent at any time by email; I then remove your feedback from the website.

8. Links to other websites

When you follow a link to another website (for example a project site), that website's own privacy policy applies.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), correction (Art. 16), deletion (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). Write to me by email. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
